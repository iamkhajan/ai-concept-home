01 · Ask Cook AI Assistant

Just say what you're craving

Alex taps the mic and says "Steak, medium rare". Cook AI Assistant builds a plan, and one tap on Send to Oven sends the mode, temperature and time straight to the wall oven.

02 · Remote preheat

Preheat from anywhere

The oven heats to 135°C while Alex seasons the steaks. The app shows the live temperature and sends a notification when the oven is ready, so nobody has to wait next to it.

03 · Recipe-guided cooking

The settings are already in

Convection Bake, 135°C, 45 minutes are all set. Alex slides the tray in, closes the door and the timer starts on its own.

04 · Voice control

Hands busy? Just ask.

While setting the table, Alex asks the kitchen assistant how the steak is doing, then adds a 5-minute sear for a better crust.

05 · Smart notifications

It tells you when dinner's ready

When the timer ends, Alex's phone and the speaker both announce it, and the oven switches off so the steak rests at a perfect medium rare.
